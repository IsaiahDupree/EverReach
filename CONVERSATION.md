## The Idea

Okay, so this is for all the health enthusiasts, health obsessed people. We don't realize how much sunlight we need. We have been so obsessed with our screens. We have been so addicted to dopamine from our screens that we forgot to completely go outside. Now, for all those people who have not touched grass recently, this app is for you.

It's called **sunlight** or a derivative of the term sunlight.

This app will basically track how much sunlight you're getting each day, how much sunlight you need to get each day based on:
- Your skin pigmentation
- Whether you're outside in a non-enclosed environment
- The weather stats and statistics of that day
- Humidity, cloud exposure, and other atmospheric conditions

And if you want to actually go tan and really concentrate how much sunlight you're getting, this app will tell you where are the best places to go get a tan in terms of natural sunlight.

This is literally the alternative to taking Vitamin D medicine. The app will tell you how many hours of sunlight you need and how much sunlight you're getting based on your location outside.

It will give you a **map** showcasing the best areas right now, as well as predicted in the future based on weather forecasts.

If you're planning a trip outside on the weekend or on weekdays, it will show you where are the best places to go during your trip to get the best sun.

**Notifications:** If you have plans for outside and the weather changes and now your plans are ruined because you expected to get sunlight but now you don't — the app notifies you.

The app uses **AI analysis**: if your skin has this pigmentation, combined with your health metrics (age, height, body weight), synced with your health data if you allow it — that will determine how much sunlight you need and how much you're actually getting.

Measurement maps, location data — all combined.

The app will also tell you not to use sunscreen... actually, you should always use sunscreen, no matter what.

**Times during the day when sunlight is at its peak** — integrated with weather data via API.

**A log** so you can log how long you've tanned or how long you've been in the sun.

**A weekly overview page** of how many minutes of daylight you've gotten, how many minutes you've tanned — some cool stats to make you feel like you did something when you went outside.

Different physiology/psychology advice, tips — a tip of the day somewhere on the app.

**Warnings when UV is really high** in specific areas.

**Pricing model:** Subscription, $3.99 a month — very cheap, on par with Snapchat+.

## AI Response — Product Expansion

### Core Product Angles

- **Daily Sun Target** — shows recommended outdoor light goal for the day
- **Best Time Window** — safest and most effective times to go outside
- **Sunlight Score** — tracks how much useful outdoor exposure you got today
- **Weekend Planner** — forecasts best parks, beaches, trails for sunlight
- **Trip Sun Map** — shows where sunlight conditions will be strongest during your trip
- **UV Risk Alerts** — warns when conditions are too intense
- **Cloud Break Predictions** — tells you when clouds are expected to clear
- **Circadian Mode** — emphasizes morning light for energy and sleep rhythm
- **Mood + Energy Correlation** — compare sunlight time with mood, sleep, and focus
- **Outdoor Streaks** — daily streaks, weekly recaps, monthly "hours outside" stats

### Premium Features
- Apple Health / Google Health sync
- Location-based exposure timer
- "You've been indoors too long" nudges
- Open-area detection — favors parks, beaches, rooftops, trails, fields
- Shade vs full-sun guidance
- Golden hour / peak daylight notifications
- Smart widgets for today's sun opportunity
- Journal/log for tanning time, walk time, and total outdoor minutes
- "Sun plan" for the day based on forecast
- Friend challenges — "30 minutes outside before noon"

### Personalization Model
- Self-selected skin profile (Fitzpatrick scale)
- Optional camera estimate
- Age
- Location
- Weather + cloud cover
- UV index
- Elevation
- Time of day
- Body areas exposed
- Sunscreen yes/no
- Health app data (opt-in)

### Core Screens
1. **Home** — today's sunlight target, current UV, best time to go out
2. **Map** — best nearby sunlight spots right now
3. **Forecast** — sunlight opportunity by hour for next 7 days
4. **Logbook** — outdoor sessions, tan time, walk time
5. **Trends** — weekly and monthly exposure stats
6. **Insights** — "You usually miss your best sunlight window on Tuesdays"

### Gamification
- Sunlight rings (like Apple activity rings)
- Weekly outdoor badge
- "Before noon" streak
- "7 days touched grass" challenge
- City leaderboard or friend leaderboard
- Unlockable milestones — "5 hours outside this week"

### Monetization
- **Free:** basic tracking, daily target, current UV
- **Pro $3.99–$6.99/month:** maps, forecasts, trend analytics, smart alerts, trip planner, health integrations
- Annual plan with discount
- Optional family plan
- Optional travel sun pack
